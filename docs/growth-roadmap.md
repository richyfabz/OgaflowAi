# OgaFlow Growth Roadmap

## Hackathon MVP

Autonomous payment and order resolution for Nigerian SMEs.

## Future Capabilities

- Sales and lead qualification
- Accounts receivable and collections
- Procurement
- Supplier coordination
- Delivery operations
- Returns and refunds
- Customer support
- Fraud and anomaly detection
- Inventory forecasting
- Business analytics

## Long-Term Direction

OgaFlow can evolve from a single operations agent into an agentic
business operations layer across voice, messaging, web, and connected
business systems.
